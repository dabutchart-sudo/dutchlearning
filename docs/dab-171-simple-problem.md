# DAB-171 — explain a simple problem

Status: in source, not on the live app. The live course remains Zin V5.1.169. A native-speaker pass of the new Dutch is still outstanding.

## This slice

S1 Explain a simple problem follows A1.25. It is the first scheduled conversational chunk, for one real situation: say what is wrong and ask for help.

The lesson teaches reusable chunks:

- `ik begrijp het niet` when you do not understand;
- `ik ben te laat`, or `de trein is te laat`, when someone or something is late;
- `mijn telefoon werkt niet` when something does not work;
- `ik heb hulp nodig` to ask for help.

A second idea can join with `en`, `maar` or `want`. Each idea keeps its own subject and finite verb. Practice introduces the chunks. The proof pool recombines them with familiar people and things. Mastery and retention stay the normal written checks. The daily 20 is unchanged.

A1.26 stays closed. Introductions, family and home, ordering, plans and directions already have course topics. Those objectives, and further chunk scenarios, are still ahead.

## Evidence

Typed and guided sentence work remains written production or recognition. This slice does not add speaking, and it does not treat a problem sentence as interaction evidence. Listening stays the existing optional exposure kind already used by earlier topics.
