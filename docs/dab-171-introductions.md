# DAB-171 — introduce yourself

Status: in source, not on the live app. The live course remains Zin V5.1.170. A native-speaker pass of the new Dutch is still outstanding.

## This slice

S2 Introduce yourself follows S1. It is the next scheduled conversational chunk, for one real situation: say who you are and where you are from.

The lesson teaches reusable chunks:

- `ik heet` for a name;
- `hoe heet u?` to ask a name politely;
- `ik kom uit` for where someone comes from;
- `ik woon in` for where someone lives.

A second idea can join with `en`. Each idea keeps its own subject and finite verb. Practice introduces the chunks with Jan, Anna, Tom and Lisa, and with Nederland, Utrecht, Amsterdam and Rotterdam. Proof also uses Den Haag and family words such as ouders, vader, moeder, zus and broer. The proof pool recombines them. Mastery and retention stay the normal written checks. The daily 20 is unchanged.

A1.26 stays closed. Ordering, plans, directions and simple problems already have course topics. Further chunk scenarios are still ahead.
